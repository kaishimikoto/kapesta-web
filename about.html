About Kapesta

Kapésta is a digital café discovery and recommendation platform specializing in café listings, business information, customer reviews, and preference-based filtering to streamline café searches and promote local businesses in Cabanatuan City.

Learn more about us

Why We Built Kapesta

Finding a café to study in isn't always as simple as picking the nearest one. We want to know if the Wi-Fi is reliable, the space is comfortable, and the prices fit our budget.


That's why we created Kapesta! We're bringing café information together in one place, helping us explore study-friendly spots around Cabanatuan City and choose one that suits our needs.

Our Mission

We make café hunting easier by bringing the details that matter closer to us. From Wi-Fi and power outlets to noise levels, amenities, and budget, we help you find a spot that feels right for studying, working, or enjoying a cup of coffee.

How Kapesta Works

1. Explore Cafés

Let's discover cafés around Cabanatuan City and find new spots worth checking out.

2. Find Your Fit

We can narrow down our choices based on needs, preferred amenities, and budget.

3. Get the Details

Let's check café amenities, operating hours, menus, and other helpful information before we visit.

4. Share Our Experience

We can rate and review cafés to help fellow café hunters make informed choices.

The Meaning Behind Our Name

For us, Kapesta is about more than finding a place to grab coffee. It's about discovering a spot where we can feel comfortable, focus on our tasks, and enjoy our own café experience.


That's the heart of our tagline, "Find Your Timpla." We all have different preferences, and we're here to help each of us find a café that feels just right.
