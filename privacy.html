Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

Introduction

Kapesta is a student-developed café finder and recommendation website designed to help users discover cafés in Cabanatuan City, Nueva Ecija.

1. Information We Collect

If our website's forms are functional, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, contact number, inquiries, feedback, and problem reports.


Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for your inquiry.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information provided through our website may be used to respond to inquiries, review feedback, address reported issues, and improve the website.


We do not sell personal information.

3. Data Protection

We will take reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Access to submitted information should be limited to authorized project members who need it for the purposes stated above.

4. Data Retention

Personal information should only be kept for as long as necessary to address its intended purpose and applicable requirements. Information that is no longer needed should be securely deleted or disposed of.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external café websites, maps, or other services. These services have their own privacy practices, which are outside our control.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to be informed, access or correct your information, and exercise other applicable rights under the law.

7. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or concerns, please contact us at:


Email: kapesta@gmail.com

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any revisions will be posted on this page.


This policy is intended to reflect Kapesta's actual data collection and handling practices and should be updated whenever those practices change.
