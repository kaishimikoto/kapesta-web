Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

Introduction

Kapesta is a student-developed café finder and recommendation platform designed to help coffee lovers, students, and visitors discover cafés across Cabanatuan City, Nueva Ecija. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when interacting with our website's contact, feedback, and issue reporting forms. This may include:


  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Inquiries, feedback, category details, and report descriptions
  • Attached files or screenshots submitted to help resolve technical issues

Note: Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for your inquiry.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected exclusively to:


  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Review user feedback and improve platform features
  • Investigate and address reported website errors

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement reasonable administrative and technical security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse. Access to submitted data is strictly limited to authorized Kapesta project team members who require it for operational and support purposes.

4. Data Retention & Disposal

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected or to satisfy administrative requirements. Once your inquiry or issue is resolved, or when the data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or disposed of.

5. Third-Party Links

Kapesta contains links to third-party café websites, social media platforms, and external navigation services (such as Google Maps). These external services operate independently and have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their content or data privacy practices.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have specific rights regarding your personal data, including:


  • The right to be informed about how your data is processed
  • The right to access, update, or correct your personal information
  • The right to object to data processing or request erasure where applicable under law

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our platform or data handling practices. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at:


  • Email: kapesta@gmail.com
  • Location: Cabanatuan City, Nueva Ecija, Philippines
